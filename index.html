Holiday Cheer: QHTA Staff Appreciation Drive

Your fellow residents are excited to announce the first-ever holiday appreciation drive, run entirely by the tenants of Quebec House. You can show your appreciation this holiday season by making one contribution that will be shared fairly among the maintenance, custodial, concierge, and package-handling staff of Quebec House.

We believe it's important to be transparent so residents understand how distributions are calculated and who will receive gifts.

Gifts will be shared evenly among staff based only on full-time or part-time status, regardless of tenure. Part-time employees will receive 75% of what full-time employees are gifted. We believe this allocation is clear and fair and reflects how tenants expect their year-end tips to be distributed.

How to donate

Donations can be made

  1. Physically via cash or check (written to Stephanie Woodrow), which you can either hand to a QHTA representative to get a receipt at one of our upcoming events, or slip under N-433 or S-____ and email qh.resident@gmail.com with what you’ve left so we have a record
  2. Virtually via PayPal or Venmo using the QR codes below

We ask that if you generously decide to contribute, please do so by December 15 so we can share our appreciation before holiday vacations.
