Quebec House Thank You Fund

A tenant-run holiday appreciation drive for the staff of Quebec House.

About

Your neighbors are launching the first-ever tenant-run holiday appreciation drive. Make one contribution to thank the maintenance, custodial, concierge, and package staff of Quebec House. It will be shared fairly among them.

Who it supports

  • Maintenance
  • Custodial
  • Concierge
  • Package handling

How it works

  • This fund is organized by the Quebec House Tenant Association (QHTA). It is separate from the landlord's holiday gift fund.
  • Contributions are divided by share: full-time staff receive 1 share and part-time staff receive ¾ share.
  • Contributions accepted: cash, check, or electronic payment.
